### Fact-Check Sources:
- **First Issue**: First 100 Rupee notes by Govt of India ~1900/1923 (RBI took over in 1938).
- **Rani ki Vav**: 11th-century stepwell, Patan, Gujarat (Mahatma Gandhi New Series, 2018).
- **Discovery**: Buried by Saraswati river, excavated by ASI in 1980s.
- **UNESCO**: Inscribed in 2014.
- **₹1 Note**: Issued by Ministry of Finance, considered a "coin" under the Coinage Act.
